About Fofanah’s Restaurant

Fofanah’s began as a family kitchen, cooking for neighbours and friends. What started with a few pots and a lot of love has grown into a restaurant that people trust for real Sierra Leonean food.

Today we serve the same recipes, made fresh with local ingredients. From rich cassava leaf and groundnut stew to spicy fried fish, every dish is cooked slowly and with care.

Whether you eat with us, collect your order or have it delivered, we want every meal to feel like it was made for you. Our promise is simple: honest food, generous portions and a warm welcome.

Our Story

Fofanah’s started in 2013 in a small family kitchen in Freetown. Our founder, Mama Fofanah, cooked her recipes for neighbours and friends, and word spread quickly. People came for the stews and groundnut soup, and they kept coming back because of how it tasted.

In 2016 the family opened a small restaurant so more people could share the food. Local farmers and market traders became part of our story, and we have worked with many of them ever since.

Today the kitchen is still run by the family, with a team who cook every dish fresh each day. We have grown, but the recipes, the portions and the welcome have not changed.

  • 2013: Started cooking from the family kitchen
  • 2016: Opened our first restaurant
  • 2020: Added delivery and catering
  • Today: Serving hundreds of happy customers every week
