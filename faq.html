Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about ordering, delivery, bookings and our food.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 am to 12:00 am, and on Sunday from 12 pm to 6 pm.

Where is the restaurant?

You can find us at Lakka High Way, Freetown, Sierra Leone. There is a map on the Contact page.

How do I place an order?

Go to the Order page, choose your dishes, tell us whether you want pickup or delivery, and press Submit. You can also call us on +232 34 821 518.

Do you deliver, and does it cost anything?

Yes. Delivery to your home or office is free inside our delivery area, and our own riders bring your food. Delivery runs Monday to Saturday, 6 am to 10 pm. We do not deliver on Sundays.

Which areas do you deliver to?

We deliver in every part of Freetown. If you are not sure whether we reach you, call us and ask.

Can I pick up my order instead?

Yes. Choose Pickup on the order page and tell us what time you want it. It will be ready for you to collect.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Call us on +232 34 821 518 as soon as you can. Once we have started cooking, we may not be able to cancel.

How do I book a table?

Fill in the form on the Contact page with your name, phone or email, date, time and number of guests. We will reply to confirm your table.

Do you cater for events?

Yes. We cook for weddings, meetings and family events. Send us a message on the Contact page with your date and number of guests, and we will give you a quote.

How do I pay?

You pay when your food is delivered or collected. Please ask us which payment methods we accept when you order.

Is your food really made fresh every day?

Yes. Our team cooks every dish fresh each day, using local ingredients from farmers and market traders. Our recipes have been passed down through three generations of the Fofanah family.

I have an allergy or special diet. Can you help?

Please tell us when you order or book, and we will do our best. We cook in a shared kitchen, so we cannot promise that a dish is completely free of any ingredient.

Why do prices change sometimes?

Our prices are in New Leones (Nle). Dishes and prices may change with the cost and availability of fresh ingredients.

Can’t find your answer?

Call us on +232 34 821 518 or send us a message.
