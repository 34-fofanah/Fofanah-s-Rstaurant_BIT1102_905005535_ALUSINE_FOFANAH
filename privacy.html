Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Fofanah’s Restaurant respects your privacy. This page explains what details we collect when you order food, book a table or contact us, and what we do with them.

1. What we collect

  • Your name, phone number or email address.
  • Your delivery address and the time you want your food.
  • Your order details, and any message or booking request you send us.

2. Why we use it

  • To prepare, deliver and confirm your order.
  • To confirm your table booking or reply to your message.
  • To call you if there is a problem with your order.

3. Who sees it

Only our team and the rider delivering your order. We do not sell your details or share them with advertisers.

4. How long we keep it

We keep order and booking details only as long as we need them to run the restaurant and answer any questions about your order.

5. Your choices

You can ask us what details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them. Call +232 34 821 518 or email info@fofanahsrestaurant.com.

6. Contact forms

When you send a request from our Contact page, your device opens your email app so you can send us the message. Your email provider handles the message in the same way as any other email.

7. Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking, and it does not load anything from outside services.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

9. Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Call +232 34 821 518 or email info@fofanahsrestaurant.com.
