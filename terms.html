Terms of Service

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Welcome to Fofanah’s Restaurant. By using this website, placing an order or booking a table, you agree to these terms. If anything is unclear, please contact us.

1. Orders and prices

  • All prices are in New Leones (Nle).
  • Dishes and prices may change with the availability of fresh ingredients.
  • Your order is confirmed when our team confirms it with you and gives you a reference number.

2. Delivery and pickup

  • Delivery is free inside our delivery area in Freetown. Our own riders deliver Monday to Saturday, 6 am to 10 pm. We do not deliver on Sundays.
  • The arrival time we give you is an estimate. Busy times or bad weather can cause delays, and we will call you if this happens.
  • Please give us a correct address and phone number so our rider can find you.
  • If you choose pickup, please collect your food at the time you chose.

3. Payment

You pay when your food is delivered or collected. Please ask us which payment methods we accept when you order.

4. Changes and cancellations

To change or cancel an order, call us on +232 34 821 518 as soon as you can. Once we have started cooking your food, we may not be able to cancel it.

5. Table bookings and catering

  • A booking request on our Contact page is not confirmed until we reply to you.
  • If you will be late or need to cancel, please tell us so we can give your table to another guest.
  • Catering for weddings, meetings and family events is priced for each event. Ask us for a quote through the Contact page.

6. Allergies and special diets

Tell us about any food allergy or dietary need when you order or book. We cook in a shared kitchen, so we cannot promise that any dish is completely free of an ingredient.

7. Our website

The text, photos and logo on this website belong to Fofanah’s Restaurant. Please do not copy them without our permission. We try to keep the menu, prices and opening hours up to date, but they may sometimes change before the website does.

8. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when they were last changed.

9. Contact us

Questions about these terms? Call +232 34 821 518 or email info@fofanahsrestaurant.com.
